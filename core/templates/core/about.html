Ntatemoholo Seromo is a Spiritual Counselor and respected Sangoma healer dedicated to preserving the rich spiritual traditions of Southern Africa. As a Sangoma, he serves as a bridge between the living and the ancestral world, helping people find guidance, healing, and a deeper understanding of life's challenges.

Having trained under experienced traditional healers, Ntatemoholo combines ancestral wisdom with practices such as bone throwing, herbal medicine, dream interpretation, and ancestral communication. His work is guided by respect for tradition and a genuine commitment to helping others find balance, healing, and direction.

The journey of becoming a Sangoma, known as Ukuthwasa is not a path that is chosen—it is a calling received from the ancestors. It is a sacred responsibility passed down through generations, requiring humility, discipline, and a lifelong commitment to serving those who seek spiritual guidance and healing.
